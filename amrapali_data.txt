=== ABOUT AMRAPALI UNIVERSITY HALDWANI ===
- Name: Amrapali University (AU), formerly Amrapali Group of Institutes (AGI).
- Established: Founded in 1999 under the aegis of Modern Academy Educational Society.
- Status & Recognition: UGC recognized State Private University (UGC Act 1956). Approved by AICTE, PCI (Pharmacy Council of India), NCTE.
- Location: Shiksha Nagar, Lamachaur, Haldwani, Nainital, Uttarakhand - 263139.
- Campus: 19-acre lush green, pollution-free campus with Wi-Fi connectivity, CCTV surveillance, 4000+ students, and 200+ faculty members.
- Official Email: admission@amrapali.ac.in | query@amrapali.ac.in
- Official Website: https://amrapali.ac.in

=== ACADEMIC FACULTIES & DEPARTMENTS ===
1. Faculty of Technology & Computer Applications (FOTCA): B.Tech (CSE, AI/ML, Data Science), BCA, MCA.
2. Faculty of Commerce & Business Management (FOCBM): BBA, B.Com (Hons), MBA (Dual Specialization: HR, Finance, Marketing, IT).
3. Faculty of Hospitality Management (FOHM): BHM, BHMCT, Diploma in Hotel Management, Diploma in Food Production.
4. Faculty of Pharmacy: B.Pharm, D.Pharm.
5. Faculty of Education: B.Ed.
6. Applied Sciences & Humanities: B.Sc, M.Sc courses.

=== DETAILED FEE STRUCTURE (APPROX. PER SEMESTER / YEAR) ===
- MCA: All India students: ~₹63,000 per semester. Uttarakhand Domicile students: ~₹47,250 per semester (25% rebate).
- BCA: ₹35,000 - ₹45,000 per semester depending on state quota and specialization.
- B.Tech (CSE / Emerging Tech): ₹45,000 - ₹65,000 per semester. Total 4-year tuition: ₹3.90L - ₹6.50L.
- MBA: Total tuition fee approx ₹1.80L - ₹4.14L across 2 years. Uttarakhand quota fee concession applicable.
- BHM / BHMCT: ~₹60,000 - ₹63,000 per year / semester depending on specific degree or diploma option.
- B.Pharm: Total tuition ranges ₹3.42L - ₹4.56L for 4 years with state domicile concessions.
- D.Pharm / Diplomas: Ranges from ₹40,000 to ₹60,000 annually.
- Note on Domicile: Students having an Uttarakhand Domicile certificate receive a 25% to 26% fee rebate on standard tuition.

=== ELIGIBILITY & ADMISSIONS ===
- MCA: Pass in BCA / B.Sc / B.Com / BA with Mathematics at 10+2 level or at graduation level with minimum 50% marks (45% for reserved category).
- BCA / BBA / B.Com: 10+2 from any recognized board (CBSE, ISC, Uttarakhand Board) with minimum 45% - 50% aggregate.
- B.Tech: 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry/CS with minimum 45% marks. Valid JEE Main scores preferred.
- MBA: Bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum 50% aggregate. CAT, MAT, or CMAT scores accepted.
- Admission Modes: Direct merit-based registration through 10+2/graduation marks, CUET scores, or campus walk-in counseling.
